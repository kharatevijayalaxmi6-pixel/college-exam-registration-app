Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: April 2024

1. Cancellations

Exam registrations submitted through the SDMCET Exam Portal app are final once processed. Cancellations of the registration are only accepted within 24 hours of the payment, provided the university's final deadline has not passed.

To request a cancellation, you must email kharatevijayalaxmi6@gmail.com with your USN and Transaction ID.

2. Refunds for Failed Transactions

In the event of a failed transaction where the amount is deducted from your bank account but the registration is not successful on the app, the amount will be automatically refunded to the original payment method by Razorpay within 5-7 business days.

3. Refunds for Cancellations

If your cancellation request is approved, a refund will be initiated. Please allow 5-7 business days for the credited amount to reflect in your bank account or credit card statement.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Registration fees are strictly non-refundable if requested after the 24-hour window, or after the official final registration deadline mandated by the university administration.
